Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Brain Hook ("we") publishes short facts videos on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy covers the small tool we use to upload our own videos to our own accounts.

What we access

The tool signs in to Brain Hook's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, only with the permission of the account owner, and only to upload and publish Brain Hook videos. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, followers or any other users.

Data we store

We store only the access tokens needed to keep the tool signed in, on a private computer controlled by the Brain Hook owner. Tokens are never shared, sold or published.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. Videos are sent only to the platform they are being published on and, for Instagram, to a temporary file link that is deleted automatically within an hour.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the account's security settings on each platform, for example Google account permissions.
